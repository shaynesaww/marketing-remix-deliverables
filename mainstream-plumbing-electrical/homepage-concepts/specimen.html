Seven directions for the Mainstream homepage

Real type, real color, real photographs. Every direction below is built from the palette already in Jacob's logo and truck wrap. Nothing here invents a color.

The words are held identical across all seven on purpose, so what you are comparing is the design and nothing else. Pick three.

Reading these

Every headline above is identical, so any difference you feel is coming from the design. What actually varies between the three you pick is the order of the first screen: one leads with the emergency and the price, one leads with Jacob himself, one leads with both trades on a single job. Everything below the fold is the same thirteen sections in the same order.

All seven use only the five colors at the top of this page. None of them introduce a sixth.

Two of these carry a real risk worth naming. The Table is beautiful and may read as a law office instead of a plumber. The Wrap is unmistakably his, and that is the problem, because it inherits the look of the flyer we are replacing.
