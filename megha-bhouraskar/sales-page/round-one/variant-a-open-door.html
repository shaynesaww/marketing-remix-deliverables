Questions

I do not have a visa. Does that stop me?

No. Owning an American company and living in America are two separate things, and people conflate them constantly. You can form it, bank it and run it from where you are sitting right now.

How much money do I actually need to start?

Less than the number in your head. There is no minimum capital requirement to form a U.S. company. Module 2 walks the real costs, line by line, so you are not guessing.

Will an American customer take me seriously if I am not there?

They already do, when the presence is set up properly. That is most of what Module 2 is for. A client of mine has not lived in the States for fifteen years and runs her U.S. affairs through me on a power of attorney.

Is this legal advice?

It is not, and I want to be direct about that. This is education. It explains how the system works and what to weigh, so you can make good decisions and ask better questions. It does not create an attorney and client relationship, and it is separate from my law practice. For your specific situation, you talk to an attorney about your specific situation.

I have a full time job. How much time does this take?

The lessons are short and they are built for a phone on a commute. The course does not expire and neither do you. Go at whatever speed your life allows.

I do not have a credit card. How would I pay for the paid room?

You would not need one to start, because the course is free. When the room opens we are building payment around how India actually pays, not how America assumes everyone does.
