The sales page, in three directions.

Same page, same words, same typefaces. What changes is the world it lives in. Open each one, scroll it to the bottom, and tell me which one feels like you.

Every direction uses the palette and the Georgia and Helvetica pairing from the visual direction brief, and the logo, the clouds and the sun arc all come out of the real logo file. The wording is identical across all three on purpose, so the only thing you are choosing here is the look.
