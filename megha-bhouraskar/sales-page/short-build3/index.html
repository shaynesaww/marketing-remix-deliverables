Questions

I do not have a visa. Does that stop me?
No. Owning an American company and living in America are two different things, and almost everybody runs them together. You can form it, bank it and run it from where you are sitting right now.
How much money do I actually need?
Less than the number in your head. There is no minimum capital required to form a U.S. company and no minimum balance to keep it alive. There are real third-party costs to set things up, and Module Two walks them so you are not guessing.
Is this legal advice?
It is not, and I would rather say that plainly than bury it. This is education: how the system works, what to weigh, and which questions are worth asking. I can show you every step. What I cannot do in a video is tell you which one applies to your situation, because that depends on facts I do not have. For that, work with a qualified professional in your jurisdiction. Joining does not create an attorney and client relationship.
I have a full-time job.
I know, and probably a demanding one with people on the other side of the world sitting in your calendar. The lessons are short and built for a phone. Nothing expires, nothing drips, and there is no cohort you can fall behind. Go at the speed your life actually allows.
