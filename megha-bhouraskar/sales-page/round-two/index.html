Three ways to build the deep blue.

You picked the midnight world on our call, so the color is settled. The open question is where the depth comes from, and these three answer it three different ways. Same palette, same typefaces, same words, so the only thing changing is how the page is built.

The difference is easiest to feel at the seams. Watch what happens where one section ends and the next begins, and notice how solid or how soft the page feels as you scroll. That is the whole question these three are asking.

1

Atmosphere

Depth from light

A dark room with a lamp just off the edge of the frame. Light pools inside each section and then falls away, so sections dissolve into each other instead of stopping at a hard line. The cream arrives once, full width, as a built edge. It reads like a documentary.

Open
2

Strata

Depth from layers and edges

Heavy sheets laid on top of each other so you can see every edge. Your portrait hangs out of the top section and onto the one below it, and the cream sits inset with midnight running down both margins. This is the most structured of the three, and it reads like a print feature.

Open
3

Horizon

Depth from your own logo

The sun and the horizon line out of your logo, used as the structure of the page. Every section sits at a different hour: dawn, then night, then daylight, then sunrise again, so the light travels as you scroll. It is the only one of the three that could not be lifted onto somebody else's website.
