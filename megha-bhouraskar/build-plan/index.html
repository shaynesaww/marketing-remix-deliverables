What it actually takes

This is not a website. It's a whole world.

To launch this the right way, a lot has to come together, and most of it has to be built from scratch, in your voice, around who you are.

You need a name and a brand. You need your books and your talks organized and turned into something teachable. You need a funnel that actually works, and a page for every step of it. You need the platform set up, the community built, the payments working from a phone in any village in India. You need the emails that stay close to people after they find you. And then the whole thing has to launch, with enough momentum that people already know about it by December.
